# First-draft review - 22 September 2026

## Scope

The complete nine-movement manuscript in [chapters/](../chapters/README.md) received two continuous critical passes from the existing *Assess the RfP* review session. The reviewer was asked not to edit files and to separate structural problems from matters that require actors.

The review tested:

- stage-readable speculative rules;
- the causal chain from the quiet trial to Ruth's departure;
- the scale and aftermath of Kit's breach;
- distinction and pleasure in the three voices;
- Helen's freedom to refuse in the ending;
- continuity, repetition, pacing and production feasibility.

This was a manuscript review, not a table read, performance or audience test.

## First-pass findings

The reviewer found a convincing family play inside the premise and recommended preserving the overall structure. Four changes were necessary before a reading:

1. Helen's familiar and expansive physical modes needed continuous tracking, especially between the rehearsal, Ruth's departure and Kit's final request.
2. Kit's use of the private memory needed to target Helen, who was preventing Ruth's departure, rather than imply Ruth still needed persuading to leave.
3. Several late exchanges supplied polished diagnoses after actions had already made the point.
4. The opening repeated its packing and waiting negotiations after their dramatic function was complete.

The reviewer also asked that Kit gain one particular pleasure or contribution in Neri's household, and that the departure establish its consequence without becoming an activation ceremony.

## Revisions made

- After Ruth leaves, Helen visibly releases the familiar presentation before Kit asks for it again. Kit must address the expansive Helen before she deliberately offers the familiar hand.
- Kit now says, "You were frightened then. You aren't now. Give her the bag." The misused memory is aimed at Helen's obstruction.
- The scarf circuit and the second round of hour-and-waiting bargaining were compressed.
- Explanatory statements about sacrifice, preservation and forgiveness were replaced with disputed particulars, unanswered questions and physical consequences.
- Helen's later breadth is limited to already established attention: both sides of the harbour, the child retelling the event, and Ruth.
- Ruth and Helen state the practical consequence of moving briefly; the sleeve, rejected help and exit carry the remainder of the departure.
- Kit had expected to burn the crust of Neri's first loaf because its smell recalled the place they met. Someone else performs the contribution badly after Kit misses the beginning.
- Kit's report to Neri no longer becomes a sequence demonstrating that they have learned the correct privacy lesson. The shared view remains closed.

## Second-pass result

The reviewer found no remaining substantive blocker and judged the manuscript ready for a table-read checkpoint. One residual prop-continuity error was found and corrected: the bag was already on the floor in movement two and is no longer moved off the table a second time.

No expensive production requirement emerged. The central practical risk remains the repeatable physical distinction between Helen's modes without light, sound or explanatory mime.

## Material to protect in rehearsal

- Helen's pleasure when a child disrupts her carefully made tide.
- The family's unpunishing laughter during Ruth's dance story.
- The potatoes exchange and its incomplete account of the original invitation.
- Ruth accepting Kit's help with her sleeve after refusing Helen's.
- Kit beginning to leave before Helen calls them back.
- The quiet trial succeeding in its narrow terms.

## Questions for the table read

1. Do listeners understand why Ruth chooses Sal after the successful quiet, without being told what to conclude?
2. Can the actors make Kit's continuing view of the water and its later withdrawal unmistakable?
3. Does Helen's widened attention after Ruth leaves remain legible into the final request?
4. Does Kit's breach change trust without taking over the play?
5. Does Ruth's exit have enough duration and weight before Kit asks Helen to come?
6. What runtime results from played silence and unhurried physical business?
