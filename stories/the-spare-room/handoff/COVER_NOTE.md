# Cover note to the writer

**Subject: The Spare Room — development package and first-draft brief**

Hello,

Attached is the development package for *The Spare Room*, a three-person science-fiction family play intended for a simple room and approximately seventy minutes of uninterrupted performance.

We would like to move into a complete first draft once commission terms are agreed. The reading packet contains a short brief, the current outline and two exploratory passages. The ZIP includes editable sources, optional character and voice notes, and the development history.

The latest pass settles two practical points: why ending Kit's invited sharing does not end Ruth's residence, and why the quiet trial makes Ruth choose departure before Helen is ready to accept its terms. The other concerns—Kit's betrayal, the individuality of the characters and the effect of the ending—are questions to discover through writing and a reading.

Please treat the material as a developed starting point with room for your judgment. The production constraints and central family relationships matter; the scene divisions, individual lines and proposed ending remain open to the play you find.

Please let us know your availability, fee structure and proposed process so that the commission, revisions, credit and rights can be agreed before work begins. We are not asking for additional unpaid scenes or another original treatment at this stage.

The suggested reading order is on the packet's opening page.
