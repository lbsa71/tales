# The Spare Room — audio adaptation

**Status: audio draft 1.0 delivered, 23 September 2026. Complete writing draft; unperformed and untested.**

The English audio drama targets approximately 45 minutes, with three character roles and sparse narration. The complete [script](SCRIPT.md), [production notes](PRODUCTION_NOTES.md) and [performed listening-test procedure](LISTENING_TEST.md) fulfil the writing milestone in [WORK_ORDER.md](WORK_ORDER.md).

The script is isolated from its supporting material: [PREAMBLE.md](PREAMBLE.md) holds the version, status, source and roles; [CONVENTIONS.md](CONVENTIONS.md) holds the general reading and sound conventions. Document layout revision 1, 23 September 2026, follows the requested split without changing the dialogue or narration.

The script contains 4,368 dialogue words and 282 narration words. Forty-five minutes remains a target: the production notes give a broad, unverified 36–45-minute planning range. A complete natural performance must establish its duration. No recording or listener test has taken place; performance, individual listener responses, revision against findings and any necessary further timing read remain outstanding.

This is an adaptation within [The Spare Room](../../README.md), with its own manuscript and development record. It is not a new story project, a recording of the stage text, or the existing Swedish first-draft audio.
