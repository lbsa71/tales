## Roles

- **RUTH:** Helen's mother.
- **HELEN:** Ruth's daughter, Kit's mother.
- **KIT:** Helen's adult child; they/them.
- **NARRATOR:** a distinct, unhurried voice reporting visible actions.

A fourth performer is preferred for narration; the production notes describe the casting proposal and a possible doubling arrangement. Sal, Neri and all other people mentioned have no recorded voices.
