# The Spare Room - first draft

Nine continuous movements for three performers, played without an interval.

## Reading order

1. [The Bag](chapter_01_the-bag.md)
2. [The Other Promise](chapter_02_the-other-promise.md)
3. [Being Here Differently](chapter_03_being-here-differently.md)
4. [The Chair Stops the Move](chapter_04_the-chair-stops-the-move.md)
5. [An Invitation Misused](chapter_05_an-invitation-misused.md)
6. [Without the Witness](chapter_06_without-the-witness.md)
7. [A Visit Rehearsed Badly](chapter_07_a-visit-rehearsed-badly.md)
8. [Ruth Goes](chapter_08_ruth-goes.md)
9. [For the Door](chapter_09_for-the-door.md)

The breaks are working rehearsal divisions, not blackouts or scene changes. The room remains visible and materially unchanged throughout.

## Cast

- **Ruth** - Helen's mother.
- **Helen** - Ruth's daughter and Kit's mother.
- **Kit** - Helen's adult child.

Neri and Sal are never heard or seen.

## Playing space

A table. Three ordinary chairs. A flask, mugs, spectacles, a coat, a scarf and a swimming bag. One entrance.

Ordinary or near-ordinary clothes. No projection, recorded voice, scenic transformation or effects cue is required. Changes in Helen's attention are performed by the actors and are not announced by light or sound.

All printed dialogue is public. When Kit shares Helen's perception, receipt is silent and readable only through attention and response. The invitation lets Kit experience what Helen is presently attending to; it does not let Kit search her mind. Ruth's residence is a different relationship: while it continues, Ruth can address Helen beyond the room even when Kit cannot.
