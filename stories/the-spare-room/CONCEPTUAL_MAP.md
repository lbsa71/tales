# Conceptual Map — *The Spare Room*

Second-draft continuity and room rules, 22 September 2026. These notes support the [manuscript](chapters/README.md); they are not an introduction for spectators.

## The literal situation

Ruth lives within her daughter, Helen. The room onstage is within Helen too. It holds a familiar place in which these three living people can meet. Helen has preserved features from memory, including a chair Ruth had given away. The room remains when nobody is using it.

Helen onstage is her own self-representation inside her mind: her idea of herself in this encounter, shaped by being Ruth's daughter and Kit's mother. She is not an external operator appearing beside a picture of her mother. Her self-image is a present act of a living person, not a younger stored Helen. The same actor can make its availability change through ordinary choices of attention; the literal premise does not require a physical transformation.

Their wider world allows long lives, shared experience and ways of living beyond the bodies visible here. Those possibilities are literal. This production does not ask actors to demonstrate a new body or an altered state of being. Its physical evidence consists of looking together, reacting to something shared, being excluded, being called back and choosing to give someone attention.

The mother's personhood is settled. There is no reveal that she is a copy, a ghost, a recording or a younger stored self. She can choose another home and continue living there. Nobody must master the mechanism to understand the move.

## Three different relationships

| Relationship | What it permits | How the play makes it visible |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary company in the room | Anyone present can hear spoken dialogue and respond. | Three people converse. All printed speech is public. |
| An invited share in Helen's current experience | The invited person receives what Helen is attending to. The invitation can be ended. | Kit asks to see; Helen agrees; they share a sightline, cold and an unspoken shoe. Helen later closes the view explicitly; Kit loses the water. |
| Ruth's residence within Helen | Ruth can address Helen beyond this encounter. Residence supplies no automatic view of Helen's memories or other conversations. | Ruth reaches Helen after an exit, when Kit cannot. The pattern repeats after Kit loses the shared view. |

An invitation is not general mind-reading. Kit cannot search, rewind or select Helen's memories. When Helen relives part of the night Ruth stayed, that fragment intrudes into experience already shared. It is partial, subjective and not a complete explanation of the original promise. Kit chooses to expose it and deploy it against Helen's present obstruction.

Closing Kit's view leaves Kit able to speak in the room and leaves Ruth's residence intact. Ruth's standing access is part of that residence, not another view Helen can simply switch off. Helen may decline to answer, but that is different from not receiving the call.

Leaving the encounter is also different from moving home. Early exits do not change residence. Ruth's final departure carries out a decision the women have made explicit; no threshold, bag, gesture or activation phrase performs the change. Afterwards Ruth must arrange contact. Visits remain possible.

## The physical score

The first invited harbour view establishes a single shared sightline towards empty space. Helen and Kit follow the same event and react to the same cold. Helen still turns and answers Ruth. More divided attention must not be played as unresponsiveness; otherwise Ruth is only asking to be heard.

Ruth's later invited trial uses this familiar activity. She tries it, finds it difficult, asks to stop and is released promptly. The actor does not need to make Helen's entire wider existence visible.

After Ruth leaves, Helen returns to the harbour activity. Kit receives nothing. At the ending, Helen sets the activity aside, turns fully to Kit and takes their offered hand. These are ordinary actions already prepared by the play, not a secret gestural alphabet. Helen can offer company without restoring the breached intimacy.

No effects, lighting state, extra voice or scenic transformation is required to carry a necessary fact. The workshop tests whether the spoken cues and actions are sufficient; evocative directions are not proof that they work.

## Revelation and its consequences

The opening gives the inequality in calling, without naming the mind-room. The harbour supplies undeniable shared experience. Near the end of the successful quiet trial, Helen says plainly that Ruth lives within her and that this room is within her too. Ruth chooses Sal after this clarification.

The chair then reclassifies an object and forces an action. Helen assumed that another home would contain the mother she expects. Ruth moves the chair and refuses to guarantee such visits. Helen takes the bag. Their two handles make the obstruction legible before anyone describes it.

Kit's breach reveals the original invitation and creates a new injury. The later private history complicates responsibility; it is not another essential rule that spectators must solve during an emotional turn.

## Private scale for continuity

The original invitation occurred 312 years before this encounter. Helen had left an eighty-year marriage with four other people. She wanted separation and then discovered how frightening it felt. Ruth kept talking during the first night; Helen asked her to remain as long as she liked.

Ruth gave up a reserved place with a travelling group of dance callers. Her sacrifice was real, as were her doubts about being one among several competent callers. She has continued living, making choices and visiting others during her residence.

Helen helps compose tides for inhabited ocean worlds. She enjoys the work, its useless beauty and being credited for it. Kit and Neri are beginning life with seven other adults. Their first meal can happen without Helen or Kit; lateness costs a shared beginning, not anybody's survival. Kit's missed loaf contribution makes that cost particular.

These are private facts, not a quota of exposition. A reference to centuries need not carry the exact number or the marriage's full structure.

## What remains unresolved

Ruth's quiet succeeds. She nevertheless chooses the chance to learn with Sal over waiting for the familiar company she wants. Helen must let her leave without a guarantee about who will return. Trying a shared experience does not settle whether either woman will like future visits.

Kit remains welcome in the household and present to Helen after doing harm. Their private view stays closed through the final exit. They cannot earn its return by explaining the lesson correctly.

Helen recalls Kit because she wants company, then chooses a limited accommodation. Spectators may disagree about its generosity, fear or future cost. They should not have to disagree about whether she had a choice or what action she chose.
