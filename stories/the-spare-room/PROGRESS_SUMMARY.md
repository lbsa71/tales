# Progress Summary — *The Spare Room*

## Current phase

**Revision 2: causal outline and continuous middle passage drafted; full play incomplete and untested.**

The producer's critique identified a real gap between the package's ambitions and its dramatic evidence. Earlier descriptions of architecture and voice as “complete” were premature. The revised materials propose stronger actions; they do not establish a successful seventy-minute performance.

## Commitments retained

Three performers; one stable playing space; ordinary objects and clothing; approximately seventy minutes without an interval.

An understandable family disagreement precedes explanation. Ruth is a living, autonomous person. Helen originally invited her to remain. Ruth's residence gives her standing access to Helen. Moving changes that arrangement without ending her life. The door retains a consistent meaning.

The opening's swimming bag and the later potatoes exchange remain useful material. Familiarity and humour are not problems to remove.

## New working decisions

| Decision | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Kit has overstated both Ruth's readiness and Helen's attendance | Gives Kit a personal stake and culpability from the opening |
| Kit's new household begins this evening and can proceed without them | Supplies an ordinary, consequential reason for today |
| Helen's broader attention is enjoyable and shareable by invitation | Lets the audience experience something worth losing |
| Kit repeats a private memory received during that contact | Makes a consequential action depend on the speculative premise |
| Ruth is ready to leave before the midpoint; Helen takes the bag | Reverses who is obstructing separation |
| Ruth intends to change at Sal's, without guaranteeing familiar future visits | Makes Helen relinquish more than a demand on her time |
| Kit misses the first gathering and must correct their false assurance | Gives their choices a cost outside the room |
| Helen leaves with Kit after choosing a familiar presentation | Tests a final act whose generosity and cost can remain disputed |

The exact elapsed years, Helen's former marriage, her work, Ruth's dancing and Neri's household are chosen continuity for drafting. They can be revised, but are no longer empty slots to be supplied after the story has supposedly been solved.

## What remains to write

The beginning needs a continuous revision incorporating Kit's misrepresentations and the live quiet trial. The first opening excerpt is retained source material, not yet integrated.

The new middle is continuous exploratory text, not all of movements three to five at a verified runtime. Movements six to nine, including Ruth's exit and the new ending, are outlined but not scripted.

The full play therefore still needs a continuous draft. Do not add another layer of explanatory proposal before testing the pages.

## Next evidence

Read the new passage with three voices before treating its physical grammar as settled. Then integrate the opening and draft through the departure. Time actual spoken work; word counts and movement allocations are not a runtime.

Ask readers separately:

1. What changed each person's intention, and what could they no longer do afterwards?
2. How did Kit obtain the private detail? What did Helen permit, and what did Kit do with it?
3. Why did Helen stop Ruth leaving after asking her to go?
4. What did Kit want that was not simply to mediate the argument?
5. What surprised you after you understood where Ruth lives?
6. Where did your sympathy shift? Which person could another reader reasonably defend?
7. What moment would you describe to someone else?

Once the ending exists, ask what actually happened before asking what it meant. Agreement on events and disagreement on generosity, injury or cost would be useful evidence. General approval or correct recall of the premise would not be enough.

## Unresolved risks

| --- | --- | --- |
| Speculation remains only a metaphor | Kit acquires and uses a private memory during invited contact | Whether that acquisition reads clearly in performance |
| New power arrives conveniently at the breach | Shared perception is demonstrated first and persists after Helen changes manner | Whether the persistent contact is legible without explanation |
| Chair revelation remains an essay | Helen takes the bag and stops Ruth's departure | Whether Ruth's chosen refusal to leave on those terms feels active |
| The voices share one rhythm | Longer work description, wandering story, laughter and unoccupied silence | Whether these variations belong to the people and sustain interest |
| Kit remains a therapist | Kit misrepresents agreements and harms Helen to advance their own need | Whether their final request remains affecting after the harm |
| Wider life becomes decorative lore | Private histories drive choices; few facts enter the pages | Whether even the selected details distract from present action |
| Ending merely illustrates repeating family patterns | Helen consciously chooses a limited accommodation after Ruth's real departure | Whether the completed scene can support more than that one reading |
